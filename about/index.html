About

About peptidecal.net

peptidecal.net is a browser-based tool that turns the vial amount and water volume you enter into milliliters (mL) and U-100 syringe marks. It is built for clear, repeatable math without a sign-up. The Guides section is a separate collection of research-reference pages, with sources shown so you can read the context behind the information. Your numbers stay in your browser while you use the calculator; there is no account to create before you can see the conversion.

What this site does

  • Turns your entered numbers into clear math. The calculator converts values you provide into concentration, mL, and U-100 syringe units. It shows the relationship between those numbers without choosing them for you. You can change an input to compare the resulting math for your own label information.
  • Explains general conversions and research context. You can use the guides to understand common conversion terms and browse research-reference charts. Where a page reports a source-based value or category, it identifies the source context instead of presenting it as a default. The original source remains the place to check its full conditions and limitations.

What this site does not do

  • It does not make personal medical decisions. peptidecal.net does not prescribe, recommend a personal dose, or replace advice from a doctor, pharmacist, or other qualified healthcare professional.
  • It does not sell peptide products. We do not sell peptides, choose products for you, or tell you what to buy. Use your own vial label and product instructions for product-specific information.
